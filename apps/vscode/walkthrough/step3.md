# Quick, Balanced or Deep: Your Choice

**Ola runs on Opengates models. No API keys to manage.**

Sign in with your Opengates account and you're ready to go. Use the model picker in the chat box to choose:

- **Quick** for small edits, questions, and everyday tasks.
- **Balanced** for most coding: complex changes, debugging, and multi-step reasoning.
- **Deep** for the hardest problems, with the most capable models.
